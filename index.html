Ghilmo Choi

Undergraduate student · School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering · Korea Aerospace University

I am an undergraduate student in Mechanical Engineering at Korea Aerospace University, expected to graduate in February 2027. I conducted undergraduate research at ASOL (Autonomous Systems and Optimization Lab) from June 2025 to July 2026, advised by Prof. Daesung Jang. Since July 2026, I have been an undergraduate intern at MLCS Lab, Yonsei University. The robotic mission execution work initiated at ASOL continues as my Capstone Design project.

My research interests lie in machine/deep learning and reinforcement learning for robotic intelligence, mobile robotics, and vision-and-language navigation. I am interested in AI model architectures, probabilistic localization, and perception-to-decision systems that connect scene understanding, behavior planning, and control on real robotic platforms.

My recent work includes on-device vision-language-model-based robotic mission execution, model-based reinforcement learning, discrete Bayes filter-based localization for vision-and-language navigation, with planned integration into a navigation pipeline, Dual EKF localization on an ERP42 autonomous vehicle, and real-vehicle autonomous parking.

Projects

  1. Model-Based Reinforcement Learning under Latent Dynamics
    MLCS Lab, Yonsei University — Undergraduate Intern, 2026.07 - Present
    poster

    Investigating model-based reinforcement learning for control under variable-delay environments. Reproducing and adapting the MACURA framework with ensemble world models, uncertainty-aware model rollouts, and SAC-based policy optimization, with a focus on robust learning under uncertain observation and action delays. Exploring adaptive control of model trust, rollout length, and model-data ratio through delay uncertainty estimation.

  2. On-device VLM-based Robotic Mission Execution
    ASOL, Korea Aerospace University / Capstone Design, 2025.06 - Present
    code video

    Initiated at ASOL and continuing as a Capstone Design project, this work develops a natural-language mission execution framework for a ROS2-based UGV platform. The framework uses an on-device VLM planner, Actor-Guard-Critic validation, RTAB-Map-based visual SLAM, object detection, structured world states, and ROS2/Nav2 control.

    Extending the mission execution framework with AirSim-based perception and planning: adopting open-vocabulary object detection models such as YOLOE, NanoOWL, and Grounding DINO on AirSim data, NVBlox for 3D reconstruction, and the OMPL library for path generation.

    In parallel, currently investigating discrete Bayes filter-based localization for vision-and-language navigation through probabilistic belief updates over candidate locations, with plans to integrate the localization module into a navigation pipeline.

  3. MORAI Autonomous Driving Development

    video

  4. ERP42 Autonomous Driving and Parking
